Trades

Every trade since 1999 that we can reconstruct in full, graded twice — on what was knowable beforehand and on how it turned out. A trade can be a sound decision that lost, or a poor one that won, and the two columns separate them. Click any row to open it in the Trade Lab.

#TradeTeamsDate ProcessOutcomeDifference

Reading these. Both columns are for the team that came out best on outcome, named in the Teams column. Process prices the trade on ratings and market conditions that existed before the season; Outcome prices it on what the contracts actually returned. Both use that era's own aging curve, price curve and win calibration, so the difference between them is what the players did rather than how the model was calibrated. Draft picks are priced by the forward model at their original slot year; protections are recorded but not their thresholds, so a protected pick is priced as unprotected and flatters whoever received it.
