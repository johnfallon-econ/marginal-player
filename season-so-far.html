Every player's season through 2026-11-06 (127 games), against what this site projected before it began. Two ratings side by side: pooled is the site's usual mPM, this season's games with the four before it, each season back counting 0.7 as much, so early on it moves slowly from last year; this season is this season's games alone, shrunk toward what his box score says, so it shows who is playing better or worse, and is noisy for the first few weeks.

Reading these numbers. mPM is points per 100 possessions against an average player. mWAR is the wins he has added so far over a replacement-level player, in the minutes he has played, on this season's rating; projected is what his preseason rating would have added in those same minutes, so the gap is what his season has added or cost beyond what was expected.
