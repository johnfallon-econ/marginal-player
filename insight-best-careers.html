Can anyone beat LeBron James?

Every season since 1999-00 on one ruler: who added the most, who did it fastest, and the one active player keeping pace with the leader.

By Ben StupendousPosted Oct 5, 2026 · Updated Nov 26, 20264 min read

Ben Taylor’s list of the best careers in league history, ordered by the increase in their teams’ probability of winning championships over their careers, all seasons combined, extended down to 40th.1 Basketball-Reference also maintains various all-time lists, including Win Shares and VORP.2 LeBron James overtook Michael Jordan as all-time leader in VORP in early 2016, just over 7 years before February 2023, the month he passed Kareem Abdul-Jabbar to become the NBA’s all-time scoring leader.

Note that this excludes all of Jordan’s time with the Bulls. All players’ mWAR since 1999-00 is included, and their mean mPM calculated for those seasons.34

In all 23 of his seasons LeBron James was worth more to his team than his salary, earning in total $2.9B more, in today’s terms.5 With 419.2 mWAR he leads Chris Paul, best of all others whose entire career is shown, by 129.3 — a gap that on its own would be the 29th-best career of 2,252.

Nikola Jokić has 200.8 mWAR over eleven seasons; LeBron James had 247.2 over his first eleven, so the difference is 46.4. Jokić’s 18.3 a season is close to James’s lifetime average of 18.2. No one else on the list with more than 20,000 minutes has as high an overall mPM (+6.3). Over 23 seasons James averaged 18.2 per season; Jokić would have to continue averaging this as an older player to overtake him.

Grant Hill’s total mWAR (35.3) is lower than any other eligible player’s in the HoF, although the board only begins in 1999-00, missing his first five. The highest total mWAR of any eligible player not in the HoF is 127.5, Baron Davis’s; then Rasheed Wallace, 124.1. There is thus a fairly neat division corresponding to HoF status. 24 of all 124 players with more than 20,000 minutes and out of action 3 or more seasons are members of the HoF. Comparing all such players in with all 100 out, those in have the greater total mWAR in nine cases out of 10.

Derek Fisher won five championships with 73.5 total mWAR, equal to the number won by Kobe Bryant, with 186.2. Chris Paul has 289.9 mWAR and no title — no one without a title has more — and his teams won 1,022 games. So, comparing all 205 players with more than 20,000 minutes on this board, greater mWAR generally corresponds to more wins and, to a much weaker extent, to more championships.

LeBron James was rated +5.8 at 22; Nikola Jokić +4.4 at 22. Victor Wembanyama was rated +5.8 at the same age and has improved each subsequent year, so already has 32.8 mWAR after only three seasons.6
