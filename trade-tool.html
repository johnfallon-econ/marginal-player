NBA Trade Analyzer

Prices any trade in free-market dollars: player surplus (projected production value − salary, with aging, durability and a new-team adjustment), draft picks as E[surplus] over landing distributions (protections priced as options), and championship equity from wins + star concentration. Tax money is a footnote, not wins.
